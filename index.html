David Herel

Co-Founder & AI Researcher, BottleCap AI
Large language models, efficient training and inference.
Co-founder of BottleCap AI and Hive Spirit Technologies.
dherel@bottlecapai.com

Hi, I’m David. I co-founded BottleCap AI, where we work on making large language models dramatically faster and cheaper to train and run.

I also co-founded Hive Spirit Technologies with Tomas Mikolov, applying language models to financial markets. My PhD research at CTU/CIIRC focuses on language modeling and model behavior; I was a research intern at NUS and helped Alquist AI place 3rd in Amazon’s Alexa Prize.

Selected experience

  1. 2025 – Co-Founder & AI Researcher, BottleCap AI. Co-founded BottleCap AI with Tomas Mikolov and Jaroslav Beck. We work on making large language models dramatically more efficient in training and inference.
  2. 2025 Research internship, National University of Singapore. Worked with Prof. Michael Shieh on LLM reasoning, architectures, and evaluation.
  3. 2024 – Co-Founder, Hive Spirit Technologies. Built LLM-driven algorithmic-trading systems with Tomas Mikolov, applying language models to market data and decision-making.
  4. 2022 – PhD research, CTU/CIIRC. Studying language modeling and model behavior with Tomas Mikolov; this work led to an ICLR 2024 notable paper on self-training collapse.
  5. 2022 – 2023 Amazon Alexa Prize finalist, Alquist AI. Helped build open-domain conversational AI for the Socialbot Grand Challenge 5; the team reached the finals and placed 3rd.
